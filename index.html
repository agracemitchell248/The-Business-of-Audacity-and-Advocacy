02
Core Teaching

From Victim to Visionary

A victim mentality is not a character flaw. It is a protective shell. It made sense. It was protecting you.

Stepping into the role of visionary doesn't ignore the hurt. It reclaims your power by deciding what that hurt is allowed to build.

Advocacy Is the Bridge

It transforms a personal wound into a public resource, moving you from passive observation into active creation of a solution.

You don't have to be healed to begin. You have to be willing to let the wound point somewhere.

"They did fire me and I didn't deserve it, but I am choosing to believe it happened for me. I am no longer a casualty of their system. I am an advocate."
